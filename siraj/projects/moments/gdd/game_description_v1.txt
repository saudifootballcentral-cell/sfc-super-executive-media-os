MOMENTS: Game Description Document

1. Game Overview

Moments is a fast-paced, highly interactive local multiplayer party game designed to be played

on a shared screen (TV, PC, or Console) while players use their own smartphones as

controllers. It eliminates the need for extra gamepads, making it instantly accessible for groups

of 2 to 8 players.

The game is structured as a series of chaotic, physics-based, and skill-driven mini-games.

Players select unique characters with distinct abilities, compete across various dynamic arenas,

and accumulate points to claim the ultimate victory on the final podium.

2. Core Gameplay Loop

The Moments experience is divided into five distinct phases, managed seamlessly by the
SessionStateManager :

1. Attract Mode: The game idles on a visually stunning screen displaying a QR code. Players

scan the code to instantly join the room via their phone’s web browser.

2. Lobby & Character Selection: Players enter their nicknames and select their heroes from
a carousel on their phones. Their chosen 3D avatars instantly spawn on the TV screen.

3. Mini-Game Roulette: The system randomly selects a mini-game. A brief “How to Play”

screen appears, explaining the rules.

4. Action Phase (The Mini-Game): The core gameplay occurs. Players use customized virtual

controllers on their phones (joysticks, buttons, steering wheels) to compete.

5. Results & Podium: After each round, scores are tallied and displayed. At the end of the

session, the overall winner is crowned on a celebratory podium.

4. The Mini-Games

The game currently features 7 fully developed mini-games, each with unique mechanics, custom
arenas, and dynamic hazards managed by the  ArenaHazardSystem .

4.1. Polar Push

Objective: Be the last player standing on a shrinking, slippery ice platform.

Mechanics: Players push each other off the edge. The ice tiles randomly collapse over time.

Dynamic Hazard: Blizzard Gust – Periodically pushes all players in a random direction.

4.2. Tank Battle

Objective: Destroy all enemy tanks to be the last survivor.

Mechanics: Twin-stick shooter mechanics. Players navigate a desert arena and fire

bouncing cannonballs.

Dynamic Hazard: Artillery Strike – Target indicators appear before massive explosions rock

the arena.

4.3. Color Clash

Objective: Paint the most floor tiles with your character’s color before time runs out.

Mechanics: Walking over tiles paints them. Players can overwrite each other’s colors.

Dynamic Hazard: Paint Cannon – Drops random massive paint bombs that overwrite large

sections.

4.4. Gravity Grab

Objective: Collect the most floating orbs that match your color.

Mechanics: Players navigate a low-gravity arena, jumping and floating to catch orbs while
avoiding wrong colors.

Dynamic Hazard: Gravity Inversion – Gravity suddenly reverses, sending players flying
toward the ceiling.

4.5. Bumper Blitz

Objective: Complete 3 laps around a chaotic neon track first.

Mechanics: Top-down racing with bumper-car physics. Players can ram each other off

course.

Dynamic Hazard: Electro Bumpers – Electrified zones appear, stunning anyone who
touches them.

4.6. Blink Shot

Objective: Shoot the most moving targets before time expires.

Mechanics: Precision aiming and shooting. Targets vary in point value and speed.

Dynamic Hazard: EMP Pulse – An expanding ring temporarily disables all player abilities

and weapons.

4.7. Wave Rider

Objective: Stay on your surfboard the longest while navigating turbulent waters.

Mechanics: Players must balance and jump over obstacles on a procedurally generated
ocean.

Dynamic Hazard: Rogue Wave – A massive, unavoidable wave hits everyone, requiring

perfect timing to survive.

5. Smartphone Controller Technology

The standout feature of Moments is its seamless “Phone-as-a-Controller” technology.

No App Required: Players simply scan a QR code to join via their native mobile web
browser.

Dynamic Layouts: The phone UI ( phone-controller.html ) automatically morphs to fit
the current mini-game. For example, it displays a steering wheel for Bumper Blitz and a
dual-joystick layout for Tank Battle.

Zero-Latency Networking: Built on a custom C# WebSocket server
( MomentsWebSocketServer.cs ), it bypasses third-party cloud servers to deliver sub-
50ms local network latency.

Client-Side Prediction: The phone interface utilizes advanced input sequencing
( lastProcessedInputSequence ) to predict movements locally, ensuring the joystick
feels perfectly responsive even if the Wi-Fi fluctuates.

6. Technical Pillars

Unity Engine: Built in Unity 6000+ utilizing the Universal Render Pipeline (URP) for high-
performance, vibrant graphics.

Procedural Visuals: Extensive use of  VFXManager  and  PostProcessingManager
ensures that explosions, dashes, and environmental effects are generated dynamically
without heavy memory overhead.

Dynamic Audio:  AudioManager  handles spatial 3D audio, procedural soundscapes, and
mini-game-specific background tracks.

Robust State Management: The entire game flow is decoupled and managed by
SessionStateManager  and  ControllerGateway , ensuring no race conditions when
players join, disconnect, or reconnect mid-game.
